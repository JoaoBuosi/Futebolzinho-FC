# Futebolzinho FC v0.10.4 — Living World Engine

The frontend is now connected conceptually to a season-level world simulation layer.

## Engine responsibilities

- player development and decline
- retirements
- generated youth
- club-to-club transfers
- market value and contract updates
- competition outcomes
- promotion/relegation candidates
- persistent world identifiers

## Important data rule

Generated players are marked with generated=true and receive generated IDs. The engine never invents a real person's identity.

Real football data remains an external reality layer. API-Football should be imported server-side, normalized, and then persisted into the career universe. After initial import, the save should evolve independently from the live provider.
